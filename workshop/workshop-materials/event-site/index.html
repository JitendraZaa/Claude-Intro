FAQ

What will I build?

In the morning you start your first project in Claude. In the afternoon you build a small web page with Claude Code, then share it in the show and tell.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes, please bring one if you can. Both hands-on sessions are done on a laptop, and wifi is provided.

Can I still sign up?

Yes, but the workshop is full, so you will join the waitlist. If a seat becomes free, it will be offered to people on the waitlist.

The ticket price is still being confirmed and will be posted here.

When should I arrive?

Doors open at 9:00. The welcome and setup session starts at 9:30, so arriving a little early gives you time to get on the wifi.
